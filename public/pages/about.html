About INYA

"Inspired by New York, the beauty of freedom and the desire for uniqueness."

Inspired by New York, INYA brings together quality, elegance and sustainability as a fashion house reflecting the beauty of freedom and the desire for uniqueness.

With its wide range of products offered in women's bags, it appeals to every style with its luxury shoulder bags, handbags, velvet and canvas models. Stylish designs are produced with real leather materials and provide perfect harmony.

Designed in our studio in Istanbul under the leadership of Can Oker and handcrafted in our luxury workshops, each piece reflects a unique understanding of art and craft. With INYA, which blends modern designs with historical tradition, each piece is not only an accessory, but also a symbol of originality and elegance.
